## Steps

### 1. Open the register page

Go to [api.novadiffusion.com/register](https://api.novadiffusion.com/register). Already have an account? Sign in at the [login page](https://api.novadiffusion.com/login) with your email and password.

### 2. Fill in your details

Enter your **email** and **password**, complete verification, and submit.

![Register page · api.novadiffusion.com/register](/docs-img/register.png)

### 3. Enter the console

You're logged in automatically after signing up. Next, [create your first key](/docs/create-key).

> **Forgot your password?** Click "Forgot password" on the login page and follow the email reset flow.
