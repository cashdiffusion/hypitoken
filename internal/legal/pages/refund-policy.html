Refund & Dispute Policy

Effective 1 March 2026 · Last updated 18 May 2026

This Refund & Dispute Policy (the "Policy") describes the circumstances under which HYPIT LIMITED ("HypiToken", "we") will refund a subscription payment, how unauthorized transactions and account-takeover incidents are handled, and how to file a complaint.

1. Standard refund eligibility

Subscriptions are pre-paid and renew automatically. You are entitled to a refund of the most recent subscription charge if all of the following are true:

  • Your refund request is submitted to support@novadiffusion.com within 14 calendar days of the charge being made;
  • You have used less than 10% of your monthly API quota for that billing period;
  • The charge is from your most recent renewal (older periods are not eligible for retroactive refunds).

Refunds are issued back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Bank or card-network settlement may add additional time before the funds appear on your statement.

2. Cancellation vs refund

Cancelling your subscription stops future charges. It does not, by itself, refund the charge for the current period. If you cancel and also wish to be refunded, please tell us in your support request — we will evaluate refund eligibility under section 1 above.

3. Unauthorized transactions and account takeover

If a charge was made on your account without your authorization — for example as a result of a stolen credential, phishing attack, or account-takeover incident — please notify us as soon as you become aware:

  • Email support@novadiffusion.com with the charge date, amount, and any evidence you have (e.g. statement screenshot, time of the suspected compromise);
  • We will acknowledge within 24 hours and open a formal investigation.

During the investigation we will:

  • Suspend the auto-debit authorization on the affected account so no further charges are made;
  • Cross-check IP address, device fingerprint, and API usage logs to confirm whether the charge is consistent with your normal account activity;
  • Coordinate with our payment processor (Stripe Payments and/or Alipay International) to gather their authorization record.

If the investigation confirms that the charge was unauthorized, we will issue a full refund within 10 business days of the determination and assist you with re-securing the account (password reset, revocation of compromised API keys, two-factor authentication setup). HypiToken will, at our discretion, additionally credit the affected user's account where the incident is determined to have arisen from a security gap on our side.

4. Service-failure refunds

If a sustained outage materially prevents you from using the Service for more than 24 consecutive hours during a paid period, you may request a pro-rata credit or refund covering the affected days, regardless of usage. Outages are tracked at status.hypit.ai.

5. Ineligible cases

  • Charges that are more than 14 days old and that do not relate to an unauthorized transaction or sustained outage;
  • Charges where the account has consumed more than 10% of the monthly quota (subscription has been substantively used);
  • Promotional discounts and credits, which are non-refundable;
  • Cancellations made after the renewal charge has already occurred, where the account has been used during the renewed period.

6. Disputes and chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback or payment dispute with your bank or Alipay without first contacting our support team, we may need to temporarily suspend your account until the dispute is resolved by the payment network. To avoid this, please contact us first — most disputes can be resolved within one business day directly.

7. How to file a refund or dispute request

Send an email to support@novadiffusion.com including:

  • The email address and account ID on file;
  • The date and amount of the charge in question;
  • A short description of the reason for the request;
  • Any supporting evidence (statement screenshots, transaction ID, etc).

You will receive an acknowledgement within 24 hours and a resolution within 7 business days. For unauthorized-transaction cases, please mark the email subject line "URGENT — unauthorized charge" so our team can prioritize it.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated by email to active subscribers at least 30 days before they take effect, and the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
