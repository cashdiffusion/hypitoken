Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

From your account dashboard, go to Billing → Manage subscription → Cancel. Cancellation stops the next renewal; you keep access through the end of the already-paid period.

I didn't authorize a charge — what should I do?

Contact support@novadiffusion.com immediately. We will investigate within 48 hours and, if the charge is confirmed unauthorized, issue a full refund and disable the compromised credential, as described in our Refund Policy.

Will I be charged again if I stop using the service?

Yes — subscriptions auto-renew until cancelled. If your account has no usage activity for 12 consecutive months we will automatically suspend the auto-debit authorization and notify you by email.
